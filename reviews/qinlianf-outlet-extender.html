Build Quality & Design

It's not a premium-feeling device — the plastic is functional rather than luxurious — but it's sturdy enough that I have zero concerns about it staying in the wall. The 3-sided outlet layout is the real design win here. Spreading the five AC outlets across three faces means you can plug in bulky adapters (like a chunky power brick) without it blocking the port next to it. That's a genuine design decision that solves a real problem, and it's not something every outlet extender gets right.

The four USB-A ports are stacked on the face of the device. They share a common charging pool, so if you're running all four at once the individual speeds will drop — but for overnight charging of phones, earbuds, and tablets, it's completely fine. Anyone needing USB-C or fast charging (18W+) should look elsewhere, but that's not what this product is for.

The back — standard dual-prong plug. Clean and simple.

Installation & Daily Use

Plug it in and it works. That's genuinely all there is to it. The fit in my bedroom outlet was snug and secure — it didn't droop or wobble. There's a screw mount hole if you want to remove the outlet cover plate and fix it permanently, but I didn't bother and it hasn't moved in weeks. The indicator LED is exactly the right brightness: visible enough to confirm the unit is live, dim enough that it doesn't bother me at night. Someone in the reviews compared it to a nightlight and that's about right — barely noticeable.

Installing it is a one-second job — just push it into the outlet.

In the wall with nothing plugged in — compact footprint, doesn't stick out far.

Real-World Use: Bedside, Office, Travel

I have mine behind my nightstand, running a lamp, a phone charger, and earbuds — with room to spare. The spaced outlet layout means even a bulkier adapter fits without drama. It runs completely silent and doesn't generate noticeable heat under normal bedroom use. Several reviewers mention using multiples of these around their home, which makes sense — at this price, buying two or three for different rooms is still under the cost of most single-purpose surge strips.

Three plugs running simultaneously — no blocking, no drama.
