Full Review

What These Actually Are

I picked these up mainly to stop my laptop charger from sliding off the back of my desk every single day — you know that thing where it just slowly slithers down behind everything and you're fishing around blind trying to find the plug? Yeah, that. Nine clips for $9.99 felt like a low-risk fix, and honestly, it was.

Fresh out of the box — manual included, nothing complicated to set up.

The One Thing to Know Before You Buy

Quick heads up on expectations: the product photos make it look like each clip is a two-piece thing you can pop open and freely reposition, kind of like a proper cable clamp. That's not quite what's happening. What you actually get is a single adhesive piece with a hole just big enough for your cable to sit in, and the "magnetic" part is really just how the clip closes around the cord — not something you pull apart and reattach elsewhere. Once it's stuck, it's stuck. Am I being too nitpicky here? Maybe a little, but if you like rearranging your desk setup often, it's worth knowing going in.

Size comparison next to a coin — genuinely compact, easy to hide if that matters to you.

Where It Actually Delivers

As an actual cable holder, this thing works. I've had it holding my laptop charger cord — which I unplug and replug almost daily to bring my laptop to class — and it hasn't budged once. The magnet snaps shut with enough resistance that it won't pop open if you bump the desk, but it's not so tight that opening it feels like a fight either. That balance is honestly the best part of the whole product.

One thing worth flagging: the adhesive is genuinely strong. Like, "you're not getting this off your desk without a fight" strong. If you're worried about your desk finish, I'd recommend picking up some removable command strips and using those instead of the adhesive backing in the box — a few other reviewers landed on the same workaround, and it makes sense if you think you'll ever want to move these around.

Stuck 6 of these to the wall — they've held up amazingly well.

Final Thoughts

For $9.99 and 9 clips, this is a genuinely solid pickup once your expectations are set correctly. It's not the freely-adjustable clip system the marketing images hint at, but as a simple, no-fuss way to keep cords off your desk and out of sight, it does exactly what it needs to. Trust me on this one.
