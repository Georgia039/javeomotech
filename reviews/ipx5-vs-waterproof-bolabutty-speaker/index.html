IPX5 vs. Fully Waterproof — What's the Real Difference?

"Waterproof" gets thrown around loosely in speaker marketing, and IPX5 isn't the same thing as fully waterproof — it means the BolaButty can shrug off splashes, rain, and the occasional drink spilled on it, but it isn't rated for submersion. Drop it in the pool and you're taking a real risk. Poolside, in the rain, at the beach getting splashed — it's fine. Underwater is a different rating entirely (IPX7 or IPX8), and this speaker doesn't claim that. With that cleared up, here's the full rundown of what it does well.

Punching Well Above Its Weight Class

Portable Bluetooth speakers under $30 often suffer from tinny sound, weak volume, or cheap plastic enclosures. The BolaButty 20W Wireless Speaker breaks that expectation immediately out of the box. Engineered with two full-range acoustic drivers and dual passive bass radiators, this compact cylinder produces a full, energetic soundscape that easily fills a room or backyard patio.

The compact, lightweight design (1.28 lbs) fits easily into your hand or outer backpack pocket.

Design, RGB Lighting, and Water Resistance

Weighing just 1.28 pounds and measuring 7.87 inches tall, the speaker is built specifically for portability. The textured exterior provides a comfortable grip, while rubberized end caps cushion it against minor drops and bumps. An IPX5 water-resistance rating ensures that poolside splashes, light rain, or spilled drinks won't ruin your listening experience.

Dynamic RGB lighting rings located at each end add visual flair and sync automatically to your audio rhythm.

The RGB light rings on either side offer multiple glowing modes, shifting seamlessly between vibrant shades like blue, purple, and green. If you prefer a distraction-free environment or want to conserve maximum battery power during long trips, a dedicated button turns the lighting completely off.

Cycles through vibrant color modes with a single press of the top light control button.

Battery Performance, Connectivity & Inputs

Equipped with Bluetooth 5.3 technology, the BolaButty pairs almost instantaneously and maintains a stable connection up to 100 feet in line-of-sight conditions. Battery longevity is outstanding — offering up to 24 hours of play at moderate listening levels (50% volume with light off). A sealed rear rubber flap protects the modern USB-C charging port and standard 3.5mm AUX audio jack from moisture and dirt.

Rear port compartment sealed behind a protective rubber waterproof door.

TWS Pairing & Everyday Utility

For those looking for wider sound coverage, buying two units allows you to link them together wirelessly via True Wireless Stereo (TWS). Once paired, one speaker acts as the left channel and the second acts as the right channel, delivering 40W of combined power for movie streaming, gaming, or larger outdoor parties. Additionally, an integrated microphone lets you answer incoming phone calls hands-free without disconnecting your phone.

Includes the BolaButty 20W speaker, USB-C charging cable, user manual, and lifetime support backing.
