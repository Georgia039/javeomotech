📝 Full Review

The Go Air Pop+ is JLab's budget true wireless entry, sitting at $24.88 with Bluetooth, a built-in mic, EQ3 sound modes, and a charging case with the USB-C cable built directly into it — you'll never misplace a separate charging cord. It's aimed squarely at people who want a reliable backup pair, a starter set for a kid or teen, or an affordable option for calls and casual listening rather than critical audio work.

The box — don't toss it right away, it hides 3 extra sets of ear tips and the manual.

Fit: The Biggest Tradeoff

The most consistent theme across reviews is the fit-versus-sound-quality balancing act. Getting a proper seal in your ear improves the sound noticeably, but a full seal also makes the audio sound muffled for some wearers, forcing a looser, more precarious fit that's more prone to falling out — especially during workouts or if you yawn or talk. Most reviewers say they stay in "more than they fall out," which is a fair way to put it — it's not a dealbreaker, but it's also not the secure in-ear lock you'd get from buds designed around ear tips that seal by default.

The compact charging case — small enough for a pocket, with the cable built in.

Touch Controls: Useful in Theory, Fiddly in Practice

Touch controls handle play/pause, track skip, calls, and voice assistant activation, and in theory that means never touching your phone. In practice, several reviewers report accidental triggers — fixing your hair and brushing an earbud can spike the volume or launch Siri unintentionally. One reviewer found the double-tap pause function unreliable roughly half the time, and only got it working comfortably after remapping the tap combos in the JLab app to change right-earbud behavior. That's a real fix, but it shouldn't be a required step to get basic pause functionality working reliably.

The JLab app — this is where you remap touch controls and switch EQ3 modes.

Battery, Charging, and Everyday Use

Battery life is where this pair earns its keep. Reviewers consistently report full workdays — one logged 5 hours of continuous use and still had 60% left — and 35 hours total with the case is a genuinely strong number for the price. The built-in USB-C cable on the case is a smart touch for never misplacing a charger, though more than one reviewer noted the cable itself is short and slightly awkward to plug in. Sound quality, when the fit is right, is described as clear for calls, YouTube, and casual music — not audiophile-grade, and max volume runs a little low for some, but perfectly serviceable for day-to-day listening and video calls.

Who Should Buy This?

The Go Air Pop+ makes the most sense as a backup pair, a hybrid-work desk set, or an entry point into true wireless for someone who doesn't want to spend $150+. Reviewers comparing it directly to AirPods and Galaxy Buds consistently note it falls short on fit security and control reliability, but comes nowhere close on price either — and for casual use, most consider that trade worth it. If secure, gym-ready fit or flawless touch controls are non-negotiable for you, this isn't that pair. If you want solid battery life and decent everyday sound at under $25, it's a reasonable pick.
