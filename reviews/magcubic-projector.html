📝 Full Review

Why the Magcubic Stands Out at This Price

I've tested a few portable projectors in the sub-$100 range now, and the Magcubic is the one that genuinely surprised me. WiFi 6 and Bluetooth 5.4 on a $54 projector is not something I expected — that's connectivity spec you'd normally pay considerably more for. Combined with Android 14, auto vertical keystone, and a 180° rotatable stand, it's a more complete package than most competitors at this tier.

Clean packaging — everything you need is in the box, minus the two AAA batteries for the remote.

🏕️

Exceptional for RVs, Camping & Travel

Weighing under 1 lb and fitting in a backpack, this is the projector that actually goes with you. Multiple reviewers use it in RVs, trucks, and tents — and the WiFi 6 connection holds up better than older wireless standards in those environments. The 180° rotation means you can project onto the ceiling of a camper without awkward mounting.

Everything in the box — projector, Air Mouse remote, power cable, and user guide. Batteries not included.

Size & Portability

Size comparison against a Pepsi can — genuinely tiny. This fits in a coat pocket.

The size comparison photo tells the whole story — this thing is compact. Smaller than a water bottle, lighter than most tablets. It's the kind of size that means you actually bring it places rather than leaving it on a shelf. I tested it in a few different spots around the room, moved it to project onto the ceiling, and the whole repositioning process took about thirty seconds. The auto vertical keystone handled the rest automatically.

Picture Quality — Best in Class for This Price

Live projection shot — sharp, colourful, and well-aligned thanks to auto keystone correction.

Back panel — HDMI port, USB, and power input. Clean and minimal.

In a properly dark room at the recommended 5.2ft / 1.6m distance, you get an 80-inch image with genuinely vivid colours and sharp detail. The 200 ANSI lumens and 2000:1 contrast ratio are real — this isn't the washed-out, dim experience some budget projectors deliver. I watched a football match on it and the motion handling was solid, fast enough for sports without obvious smearing.

Football match in action — motion is smooth, colours punchy. Held up well for fast-paced content.

⚠️

Streaming Apps Are Hit or Miss

Netflix doesn't work natively on the built-in Android — it has the app icon but it won't play content. YouTube and other apps load fine. Several reviewers solved this by pairing a Roku or Fire Stick via HDMI, which works perfectly. If Netflix is essential to you, budget an extra $15 for a streaming stick and treat this as a display device rather than an all-in-one streamer.

The Air Mouse Remote — A Genuine Upgrade

Most mini projectors ship with a basic directional remote that makes navigating menus tedious. The Air Mouse remote here is a meaningful step up — you move the remote physically and the cursor tracks it, like a Wii remote. Voice search works too, which fixes the letter-by-letter search problem that plagues most projectors at this price. It's the kind of quality-of-life detail that shows the product has been thought through beyond just the specs sheet.

Audio — Better Than Average, Still Has Limits

The 5W SoundBase speaker is noticeably better than the typical thin single speaker you get in most mini projectors. For RV use or a small room with you close to the unit, it's genuinely usable. For a larger space or proper movie audio, pairing a Bluetooth speaker via Bluetooth 5.4 is the move — and the connection is stable and quick. One reviewer noted their JBL speaker paired and sounded excellent this way.

A Few Things to Know Before Buying

The power cord is short — an extension cable is worth picking up if you plan to use it in a fixed spot. The remote needs 2 AAA batteries which aren't included, so have some ready. The unit can run warm in enclosed spaces — leave it in open air rather than tucking it against a wall. And keep firmware updated as Magcubic pushes improvements regularly.

The Verdict

At $54, the Magcubic is the most complete portable projector I've come across at this price. WiFi 6, Bluetooth 5.4, Android 14, auto keystone, Air Mouse remote, 180° rotation, and under 1 lb. The native streaming app situation is its one real limitation, and it's easily solved with a cheap streaming stick. For outdoor use, RVs, travel, bedrooms, or anyone who wants a genuinely portable home cinema setup without spending £300+, this is the one to get.
