Privacy Policy

How JaveomoTech handles your data, affiliate links, and cookies.

Last updated: June 2025

Who we are

JaveomoTech (javeomotech.com) is a personal tech review website run by Geo. The site publishes honest reviews of gadgets and consumer tech products, alongside YouTube content at @JaveomoTech.

Information we collect

JaveomoTech does not collect, store, or process any personal information from visitors. We do not run contact forms that store submissions, email newsletter signups, user accounts, or any data collection system of our own.

If you contact us via email (see the Contact page), your email address is only used to respond to your message and is not shared with any third party.

Affiliate links — Amazon Associates

JaveomoTech is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

When you click an Amazon link on this site and make a purchase, Amazon may use cookies to associate your purchase with JaveomoTech. This allows us to earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. The Amazon Associates tag used on this site is javeomotech-20.

Amazon's own privacy policy governs how they handle your data. You can read it at amazon.com/privacy.

Cookies

JaveomoTech itself does not set any cookies. However, third-party services embedded on or linked from this site may set their own cookies:

  • Amazon — sets cookies when you click affiliate links, used to track purchases for commission purposes.
  • YouTube — review pages embed YouTube videos. YouTube (owned by Google) may set cookies when you interact with embedded players.
  • Google AdSense — if advertising is displayed on this site, Google may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can opt out via Google's Ad Settings.
  • Google Fonts — this site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address as part of serving the font files.

Google AdSense

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings. For more information on how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

Third-party links

This site contains links to external websites, primarily Amazon product pages. JaveomoTech is not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any site you visit.

Children's privacy

JaveomoTech does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The site is intended for general audiences.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
